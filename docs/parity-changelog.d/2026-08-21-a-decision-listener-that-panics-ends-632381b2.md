A decision listener that panics ends its turn instead of unwinding into whoever asked for it (`TurnError::Plugin`, TC-PORT-REG-5 and -6, TC-FAULT-10), closing the last containment item in the `core/*` gap. The bus is unchanged and `serial` and `waterfall` are still loud on purpose: a listener that decides something is not instrumentation, and a caller that asked one question should hear that it could not be answered. What changed is the blast radius. Before this the panic escaped `run_turn`, so the journal kept an unbalanced `turn/start` and the session was wedged open - a reader could not tell the turn was over, and the next `session.create` synthesized `interrupted` closers for a turn nothing had interrupted. The turn now fails the way any other failed turn fails, with `step/end` and `turn/end {stop_reason: "failed"}` on the journal, and the engine keeps its turn numbering so the next turn is ordinary. `AssertUnwindSafe` over the step loop is deliberate and narrow: a panic leaves `Progress` describing what the turn had done when it stopped, which is exactly the value the closers need. A mutation check confirms the suite bites: removing the containment fails TC-PORT-REG-5 and -6.
