The approval seam implemented (`crates/turn/src/approval.rs`) and ported (TC-PORT-APPR-1..12, TC-PORT-REPAIR-11..13), serving the engine half of contract section 4.4.7. Until now every registered tool ran the moment the model named it, and the only protection a deployment had was not registering the tool. Three rules carry it, and each is a defect if it is quietly relaxed: the seam fails closed, so a grant is one specific word from an answerer that ran and returned and everything else denies; the `never` policy is applied by `request` itself rather than by a listener, because a listener registered later could be ordered ahead of a gate listener and answer first, which is what upstream's three `prepend` cases exist to pin; and the audit pair is one to one, so a `never` refusal still writes both halves and the journal says a question was put rather than saying nothing happened. The policy is a fold over the journal, so a resumed session is under the policy it was under with nothing to replay but the log. `waterfall` stays loud on the bus, so the containment of a panicking answerer is this seam's own: a question that cannot be answered has a defined answer, and unwinding would fail the turn rather than deny the call. Crash repair grows one closer to match, `cancelled` rather than `unavailable` because nobody was found to be missing - the process holding the question died - and it is written before the `tool/result` of the call it was about, which is the order a live turn writes them in. Nothing calls the seam yet: wiring it into the tool pipeline and serving `approval.set` are the next slice.
