A credential typed at a terminal is no longer written down (`Tool::recorded`, TC-TOOL-RECORD-1..4, TC-PORT-TERM-42). Raised by the presentation lane, who analysed it properly and ruled out the obvious wrong fix: `submit: false` is not a password signal, and screen-only redaction would hide what the journal keeps rather than remove it. The shape built is theirs - a `secret` flag honoured at record time - with three changes from the note. The seam is a `Tool` method rather than a case for one tool's name, because the engine must not know what an argument means and because `shell`/`shell_run` leak the same way through a command line. Their second option, the engine detecting a password from the `[wait: stdin_read]` sequence, is not built and the reason is measured rather than asserted: the reliable signal is the tty's `ECHO` flag, `tcgetattr` on the pty master reads identically before, during and after a real `read -s` prompt, and this crate's own `stty -echo` at session start would have erased the transition anyway - a detector that fails silently in the safe-looking direction is what the note rejects on the presentation side. And their third option ships *with* the first rather than instead of it, because a flag the model forgets is a leak and the floor has to be stated where the model reads it. Writing the case found a second leak nobody had named, which is why it asserts on the whole journal rather than on `tool/call`: the model said the password, so `assistant/message` and the streamed `assistant/chunk` carried it too, and redacting the call while keeping the message it came from would have been a fix that fixed nothing. Failing closed here means silence - a redactor that panics withholds every argument, the opposite direction from `ToolMode`'s fail-closed and consistent with it, since a broken classifier costs concurrency and a broken redactor costs a credential. Only the named argument goes: the tool, the session and the moment stay, because an audit that cannot see a secret was typed is not an audit. `docs/contract-updates/shell-recorded-arguments.md` carries the one thing the boundary has to settle - §4.3's "the sentinel is not proof" warning is weaker than what is now true for an engine-minted argument, and the honest fix is the flag §4.3 already defers.
