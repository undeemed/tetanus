The tool-result pruner's content transform ported (`crates/turn/src/prune.rs`, TC-PORT-PRUNE-1..9), opening the `compaction/*` row. Tool results are the part of a session that grows without limit - one read of a large file can cost more context than the conversation around it - and nothing bounded them. The transform keeps the head and the tail, which is where the information is, and says in the middle that something was removed; it has no summary because a summary needs a model, and that would make it non-deterministic, unreplayable and billable. Two rules carry it. Measurement and slicing are both in characters, never bytes: the offsets come from a budget rather than from the text, so they land mid-character routinely, and the obvious implementation panics on a UTF-8 boundary - the mutation check confirms it does. And a budget whose head, marker and tail exceed its own threshold is refused where it is set, because a prune that emitted more than it accepts would make the context larger and would never converge for a caller that prunes until it fits. Applying it to a turn's history, and the durable record of a prune, are the next slices; nothing calls it yet.
